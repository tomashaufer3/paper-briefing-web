Male Dominance and Cultural Extinction

NEP-EVO CESifo Working Paper Series Eleonora Guarnieri, Ana Tur-Prats · 2026

Using the Ethnologue and a Male Dominance Index across languages worldwide, Guarnieri and Tur-Prats show gender-egalitarian societies faced higher historical extinction risk because male-dominant cultures were more militarily and demographically expansionist — a striking long-run test of institutional persistence.

Spreading Out Across Expanding Idea Space

NEP-GRO Ina Ganguli, Jeffrey Lin, Vitaly Meursault, Nicholas F. Reynolds · 2026-07

Ganguli, Lin, Meursault and Reynolds apply validated neural-language-model similarity measures to the full text of 11 million US patent claims since 1836 to document a two-century decline in inventive collisions, a good example of new extraction techniques unlocking long-run historical data.

NEP-GRO CESifo Working Paper Series Matthew Collins, Patrick Wing McHale, Devon Spika · 2026
