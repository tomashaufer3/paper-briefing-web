Carbon Taxation, Firm Performance, and Labor Demand

NEP-PBE Working Paper Series Karlsson, Jimmy · 2026-08-24

A difference-in-differences design on a 2011-2018 Swedish carbon-tax reform, using matched employer-employee data, finds a roughly 30% emissions cut alongside employment losses concentrated among older, less-educated workers - solid applied DiD identification, though the substantive topic (climate policy) sits outside the core interests.

The Making of the German Model of Industrial Relations

NEP-HPE Rationality and Competition Discussion Paper Series Felix Kersting, Iris Wohnsiedler · 2026-07-30

Using newly assembled county-occupation data on strikes, union membership and bargaining in Imperial Germany around 1900, and instrumenting union strength with distance to headquarters, shows durable collective-bargaining institutions emerged where a labor faction could both sustain conflict and credibly commit to settling it - a clean case of historical political economy with genuine identification.
